02 · Vision and mission

අපි යන්නේ කොහෙද?

Vision and mission in ordinary words.

දැක්ම · Vision

We want to lead Sri Lankan bakery and food manufacturing by making, distributing and marketing the best quality products so families get what they need. A big name is not enough.

මෙහෙවර · Mission

Happy customers, fair relationships with our people, and fresh healthy food — with less waste and an honest price.

1. Fresh

Baked in the morning. Yesterday leftover is not called fresh today.

2. Fair

The price lives in the catalogue. Nobody can type Rs. 1 in the browser and take a cake.

3. Together

Farmers, staff, outlet, rider, accountant — one process. Not 200 chats.

තේරුම: විශාල වෙන්න ඕන. ඒත් පළවෙනි loaf එක අමතක කරලා නෙවෙයි. Mission එක poster එකක් නෙවෙයි — උදේ 6ට oven එක දාන හේතුව.

11 · Checkout — the digital counter

Order එක ලියවෙන්නේ කොහොමද?

Customer tells the truth of the order

  • Name — so the box has a person, not a ticket number only
  • Valid Sri Lankan mobile (07xxxxxxxx) — so a rider or outlet can call
  • Email optional — confirmation if SMTP is on
  • Area / address — delivery
  • Mode: Delivery or Collect
  • Outlet picker, search, nearby (Uber-style map tools)

The bakery’s computer decides the bill

  • Each line matched to a real product id/title
  • Unavailable and zero-price items rejected
  • Qty 1–99, not a joke number
  • Discount only if login + phone rules pass
  • COD → status pending
  • PayHere → status awaiting_payment until the bank speaks
  • Phone saved on the account if they had none

13 · PayHere — why it is safe to bake

Five locks on the money

1Lock the total

Server creates the order in LKR from catalogue prices. The form is signed.

2Pay on PayHere

Customer leaves your page. They pay on PayHere’s site. Sandbox now; live with your merchant ID.

3Bank calls us

PayHere POSTs to /api/payments/payhere/notify. Not the customer’s browser.

4Three checks

MD5 signature. Amount equals our total. Currency is LKR. Cancelled orders refused.

5Then we bake

paid + confirmed. Email if SMTP is on. Kitchen may start. Return page tells the family the truth.

If they cancel or fail

Not paid. Kitchen does not move. They can try again or choose COD. No silent “maybe”.

If they cancel after paying

Only before preparing/delivered. Status becomes refund_pending. Accounts know money must go back. It is not forgotten in a chat.
